# Glizzanator Bot Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Glizzanator Bot** (the "Bot") processes information when it is installed in or used within a Discord server. The Bot is operated by the Glizzanator Bot developer (the "Operator").

This policy applies only to information processed by Glizzanator Bot. Discord and other third-party services have their own privacy policies and practices.

## 1. Information the Bot Processes

Depending on which features are enabled and used, the Bot may process and store the following categories of information.

### Discord account and server identifiers

The Bot may process:

- Discord user IDs;
- usernames and display names;
- avatar URLs;
- Discord server (guild) IDs;
- Discord channel IDs and channel names; and
- timestamps associated with activity.

These identifiers are used so activity can be associated with the correct user, server, or channel.

### Message activity metadata

For message statistics, the Bot may store:

- user ID;
- username;
- channel ID;
- server ID; and
- the time the message was created.

**The Bot does not intentionally store the contents of Discord messages in its activity-statistics database.**

### Voice-channel activity

For voice statistics, the Bot may store:

- user ID and username;
- voice channel ID and name;
- server ID;
- voice join and leave times; and
- calculated voice-session duration.

The Bot does not record or store voice audio.

### Streaming activity

When Discord reports that a user is streaming, the Bot may store:

- user ID and username;
- channel ID and channel name;
- server ID;
- stream start and end times; and
- calculated streaming duration.

The Bot does not record or store the content of a livestream.

### Game presence and game-session activity

When Discord presence information makes a game or activity available to the Bot, it may store:

- user ID and username;
- server ID;
- game or activity name;
- game-session start and end times; and
- calculated duration.

The Bot may also cache game metadata or artwork obtained from third-party game-data services for display on server-stat cards.

### Member-join information

To support welcome features and the "Latest Member" server-stat panel, the Bot may store:

- user ID;
- username;
- display name;
- avatar URL;
- server ID; and
- join time.

### Glizzified names and administrative adjustments

The Bot may store Glizzified-name assignments and administrative changes made to activity displays or tracked values, including the Discord IDs and usernames of administrators who make those changes when needed for audit history.

### Operational information

The Bot may maintain operational logs such as errors, restart events, command timing, API failures, or diagnostic information needed to operate and troubleshoot the service. Logs are not intended to collect message contents.

## 2. How Information Is Used

Information is processed only as reasonably necessary to operate and improve the Bot, including to:

- generate user and server statistics;
- calculate message, voice, streaming, and game activity;
- create welcome, leaderboard, user-stat, and server-stat cards;
- identify the latest member to join a server;
- assign configured roles or nicknames when enabled by a server administrator;
- preserve Glizzified-name assignments;
- provide server-administration tools;
- diagnose errors, outages, and performance issues;
- prevent abuse and maintain security; and
- comply with Discord platform requirements and applicable law.

The Operator does not sell personal information or use Bot activity data for third-party advertising.

## 3. Third-Party Services

The Bot relies on Discord to provide the account, server, channel, presence, and activity information necessary for its features.

For certain game artwork or metadata, the Bot may query **IGDB**, a Twitch service. Game-data lookups are generally based on game titles or game identifiers rather than a user's private Discord message content.

Third-party services process information under their own terms and privacy policies. The Operator does not control those services.

## 4. Data Storage and Retention

The Bot stores operational data in its application database and related runtime files. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of data and the statistical feature that uses it.

The Operator intends to retain Discord API data only for as long as it remains reasonably necessary for the Bot's stated functionality, security, troubleshooting, or legal obligations.

Data will be deleted or updated when required by Discord, applicable law, or a valid request from the applicable user, and when retention is no longer necessary for the Bot's permitted functionality.

Removing the Bot from a Discord server does not necessarily cause all historical records to disappear immediately. A server owner, administrator, or affected user may request deletion as described below.

## 5. Data Access, Correction, and Deletion Requests

A Discord user may request deletion or correction of information associated with their Discord account where applicable.

A server owner or authorized administrator may also request deletion of server-associated Bot data when appropriate.

Requests should include enough information to identify the relevant Discord account or server, such as the Discord user ID or server ID. Do not send passwords, bot tokens, or other authentication secrets.

Requests may be directed to the Glizzanator Bot Operator through the official support/contact method listed for the Bot or through the administrator of the Discord server in which the Bot is installed.

The Operator may need to verify that the requester is authorized to make the request before modifying or deleting data.

## 6. Data Sharing

The Operator does not sell Bot user data.

Information may be disclosed only when reasonably necessary to:

- operate the Bot through service providers or infrastructure used on the Operator's behalf;
- comply with a lawful request or legal obligation;
- protect the security, rights, or safety of users, the Bot, Discord, or others; or
- follow a user's express direction where permitted.

Discord API data will not be shared with third parties except as permitted by Discord's applicable developer terms and policies.

## 7. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect Bot credentials and stored data, including keeping sensitive application credentials outside public source control.

However, no system can guarantee absolute security. Users and server administrators should report suspected security issues through the Bot's official support/contact method.

## 8. Children

Glizzanator Bot is not intended to expand access to Discord beyond the minimum age permitted by Discord or applicable law. Users must satisfy Discord's eligibility requirements to use Discord and the Bot.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the Bot's features, data practices, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this policy will be revised when material changes are published.

## 10. Contact

Questions about privacy, stored information, or deletion requests may be directed to the Glizzanator Bot Operator through the official support/contact method listed for the Bot or through the administrator of the Discord server in which the Bot is installed.
